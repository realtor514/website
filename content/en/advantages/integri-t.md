The hidden defect is the best-documented nightmare in the Quebec real estate market. Water infiltration behind a finished wall. A foundation that is shifting. A non-compliant electrical system covered up by a cosmetic renovation.

The Civil Code of Quebec is clear: the seller must warrant the buyer against hidden defects. In practice, enforcing that right usually means an expert report, a formal demand, a lawyer, and sometimes three years of proceedings for an uncertain result. On both sides, everyone loses.

**Integri-T** was designed to avoid that scenario.

## What it is

Integri-T is RE/MAX Québec's exclusive legal warranty program, offered in partnership with **ACQ Résidentiel**, which has administered warranty plans for more than 30 years.

The program provides financial protection of up to **$50,000** over a period of **3 years** following the signing of the notarial deed. It applies to the buyer and the seller alike, and it is transferable to the next buyer if the property is resold during the coverage period.

## What is covered

The list of protected elements covers most of what is expensive to repair:

- Roofing, attic and insulation
- Building envelope
- Foundation and walls
- Interior and exterior cladding, subfloor
- Doors and windows
- Electrical systems
- Plumbing
- Heating and ventilation
- Heat pumps
- Water infiltration, mold and premature wear of materials

These are not theoretical categories. They are the items that actually show up in inspection reports and in hidden defect lawsuits.

## What it changes for the buyer

You are buying a property that is 20, 40 or 60 years old. The pre-purchase inspection was done properly, but an inspector cannot see behind a wall, under a slab or inside a closed cavity. That is the structural limit of the exercise.

With Integri-T:

- You have **financial security** in the event of a hidden defect claim.
- You avoid court costs and duelling expert reports, which often exceed the cost of the repair itself.
- You know, before signing, what the ceiling of your financial exposure is.
- If you resell within the three years, the warranty follows the property and becomes a selling point.

## What it changes for the seller

This is the part many sellers do not realize.

- The warranty **attracts more buyers, faster**. A buyer hesitating over an older property becomes a reassured buyer.
- It **justifies your price**. You are not discounting your property to compensate for uncertainty.
- It **limits your liability to the first $5,000** when the buyer also holds the coverage. Beyond that, the program takes over.
- It reduces your financial obligations regarding defects you did not know about yourself.

Selling a 45-year-old house without a safety net means accepting years of exposure to a claim whose timing and size you do not control. Integri-T closes that door.

## Eligibility conditions

Three rules to remember, and the third is the one that costs people the program every year.

**1. Residential properties only.** The program does not apply to commercial buildings.

**2. The building must be at least 5 years old.** Newer construction falls under other warranty regimes.

**3. The warranty must be put in place BEFORE the notarial signature.** Once the deed is signed, it is too late. There is no catching up.

The program also does not apply to properties sold **without legal warranty**, at the buyer's own risk. If the promise to purchase contains that clause, Integri-T cannot step in.

Approval takes roughly **five business days**. That is quick, but it means this cannot be an afterthought the day before the notary.
