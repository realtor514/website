Choosing a broker is not only about choosing someone who will list your property or open doors for you. It is about choosing the structure behind that person.

## What you get when you work with me

| Program | What it covers | Value to you |
|---|---|---|
| **Tranquilli-T** | Unexpected events between the accepted offer and the notarial deed, plus legal assistance | Unlimited legal consultations, free of charge |
| **Integri-T** | Hidden defects after the purchase | Up to $50,000 for 3 years, guarantee paid by the seller |
| **Coproprie-T** | Specialized training in condominium law | A broker who understands divided and undivided co-ownership |
| **My RE/MAX Discounts** | Partner discounts after the transaction | Savings on your moving-related purchases |

Three of these programs cost you nothing. Integri-T is the exception: it is a paid guarantee, offered to owner-sellers for a base amount of $1,050 payable when the guarantee certificate is issued, plus $150 per additional unit for a condominium. Rates can change without notice.

These four programs are not sales talk. They are tools I use to reduce your risk. A buyer who discovers water infiltration six months after closing does not need reassurance, they need coverage. A seller facing a hidden defect claim does not need encouragement, they need a lawyer on the phone.

Take the time to read each page. You will see exactly what is covered, what is not, and when the program has to be put in place.
