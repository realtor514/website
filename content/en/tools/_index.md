Six calculators, set to Quebec rules, answering the questions that come up
before every purchase and every sale. They run in the browser: nothing you
type is sent anywhere or stored.

## What each one answers

The **mortgage calculator** gives the monthly payment, the CMHC insurance
premium when the down payment is under 20 %, and the total interest cost over
the life of the loan. It is the starting point of almost every financing
conversation.

**Affordability** works the other way around: from income, debts and down
payment, it shows the realistic price range. It applies the two ratios
Canadian lenders use, GDS and TDS, along with the stress test.

**Transfer duties**, which everyone calls the welcome tax, are calculated
against the current brackets and the municipality. Montreal applies its own
upper brackets, which changes the amount by several thousand dollars on the
same property.

**Closing costs** gather what gets added to the purchase price: notary,
inspection, tax adjustments, title insurance, GST and QST on the CMHC premium.
This is the item first-time buyers underestimate most often.

**Rent versus buy** compares, over a chosen horizon, what renting costs and
what buying costs, accounting for appreciation, property taxes, maintenance
and the opportunity cost of the down payment.

The **home value estimator** gives an order of magnitude for market value
based on the area, the property type and its characteristics.

## What they do not do

None of these tools replaces a mortgage pre-approval or a certified appraisal.
They exist to frame a discussion, not to settle it. A lender looks at your
full credit file; a market valuation rests on recent comparable sales that
only register access lets you select properly.
