This estimator gives an order of magnitude, not a value. It multiplies the living area by a price per square foot specific to each of the six sectors offered, then applies adjustments for property type, bedrooms and bathrooms, year built, condition, parking and basement. It shows a median and a range of plus or minus 15%.

Example: a 900 sq ft condo in Laval, 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, built in the 1990s, average condition, exterior parking. No adjustment applies: $390 × 900 sq ft = $351,000, with a range of $298,000 to $404,000.

**What the tool does not see.** Its coefficients come from no published source and do not follow recent sales. It ignores the street, the lot, orientation, the quality of renovations, known problems with the building and current market conditions. To put the figure in context, compare it with the median prices Centris publishes for your city: in Laval, in the third quarter of 2026, $630,000 for a single-family home and $439,999 for a condo. A median describes all sales, not one particular property.

**Estimate, comparative analysis, appraisal: three different things.**

- **The estimator** is an automatic calculation. Its result is neither an appraisal nor a comparative market analysis.
- **The comparative market analysis (CMA)** is prepared by a real estate broker. According to the OACIQ, the broker must carry out a comparative analysis with similar properties recently sold in the same area and give a well-founded, reasoned opinion. It takes into account location, dimensions, year built, state of upkeep, number of rooms, materials and the market. The result is an estimated market value.
- **The appraisal** is prepared by a chartered appraiser (évaluateur agréé), a member of the Ordre des évaluateurs agréés du Québec (OEAQ). The appraiser gives an objective, reasoned opinion of value and presents it in an appraisal report. Membership in the Ordre is required to use the title.
- **The municipal roll value** is used for property taxation. It can differ from the market: Laval applies a comparative factor of 1.032 in 2026 to bring it to market value when calculating the welcome tax.
