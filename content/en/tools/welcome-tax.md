This calculator applies the brackets of the Act respecting duties on transfers of immovables and, for Montreal, Laval, Quebec City, Gatineau, Terrebonne, Boisbriand, Sainte-Thérèse, Delson and Saint-Bruno-de-Montarville, the municipal rates in force on the portion above $500,000. Choose the year of the notary signing: the thresholds change on January 1, 2027.

| Tax base | Rate | 2026 thresholds | 2027 thresholds |
|---|---|---|---|
| First bracket | 0.5% | up to $62,900 | up to $64,400 |
| Second bracket | 1% | $62,900 to $315,000 | $64,400 to $322,600 |
| Above | 1.5% | over $315,000 | over $322,600 |

Examples at the provincial rates, in 2026 then 2027: $2,685.50 and $2,678 for $300,000; $4,110.50 and $4,065 for $400,000; $5,610.50 and $5,565 for $500,000. Montreal's brackets, the Laval example and the payment timeline are explained in the guide [welcome tax in Quebec in 2027](/en/articles/welcome-tax-quebec-guide/). First-time buyers can recover up to $5,875 of this amount: [first-time buyer tax advantages](/en/articles/first-time-buyer-tax-credits/).
