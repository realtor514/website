## What happens after your message

Every request gets an answer within 24 hours, on weekdays and weekends alike.
The first reply is written, not automatic: it addresses your situation and
proposes a concrete next step, a call or a meeting depending on what is useful.

The first meeting is free and carries no obligation. It creates no brokerage
contract: that arises only from a signature on the mandatory OACIQ form, never
from a call or an email.

## What is worth writing

For a sale: the address, the property type and the timeline you have in mind.
For a purchase: the area, the budget and the constraints that genuinely matter,
school, transit, number of bedrooms. For a valuation: the address is enough.

The more precise the request, the more useful the first reply. None of it is
mandatory: a plain "I would like to understand where the Laval market stands"
gets a real answer.

## Other ways to reach me

The phone remains fastest for a short question. Online booking shows real
availability and avoids back and forth. The Laval office can be visited by
appointment.
