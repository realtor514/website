Boucherville is one of the most stable real estate markets in Greater Montreal. Corrections here are generally less severe than elsewhere, but the entry price excludes part of the buyer pool.

## What supports prices

The city was developed with rigorous planning: commercial arteries are concentrated, residential sectors kept their green space, and the Îles-de-Boucherville national park is accessible directly from the city.

Add to that a population whose median income is among the highest in the region, which supports demand in the upper segments and makes the market less rate-sensitive than cities where the marginal buyer is at the edge of their capacity.

## The sectors

**Vieux-Boucherville** is the heritage core: older properties, narrow streets, river proximity. The character is real and cannot be reproduced, but it comes with the realities of older construction.

**Harmonie, Sabrevois and Seigneurie** are the family sectors built mostly between 1975 and 2000. This is the most active segment of the city, with the most reliable comparables.

**The riverfront sector** is a market of its own, with fewer buyers but steady demand. Shoreline buffer constraints must be verified property by property.

## What to check here

**System age** in the 1970s and 1980s sectors: roof, windows, French drain. A property with several systems reaching end of life at once is not worth what an identical property is worth after that work is done.

**The urban planning by-law** in Vieux-Boucherville, before any extension or facade modification project.

**The in-ground pool history** when there is one: it is the item whose refurbishment cost surprises buyers most.

## Selling in Boucherville

Buyers have high expectations and plenty of ways to compare. A decluttered property, with visible minor repairs done and professional photography, sells considerably better than a comparable property presented as is.

The comparative analysis is prepared at no charge and tell you honestly what needs correcting before listing.

## Buying in Boucherville

The main risk here is the emotional purchase. The city shows well, and a spring afternoon visit rarely gives an accurate picture of what you are buying. Do the full inspection, without exception.
