Sainte-Thérèse is one of the few Lower Laurentian cities where the rental market genuinely matters. That is what sets it apart from Blainville or Rosemère, which are dominated by owner-occupied property.

## A two-speed market

On one side, a single-family and semi-detached market driven by families looking for the trade-off between price and access to services.

On the other, an income property market supported by Cégep Lionel-Groulx, the Saint-Jérôme line station and a stable tenant base. Entry prices are lower than in Laval, which attracts investors who do not live in the area.

These two markets do not respond to the same signals, and a seller's strategy differs depending on which side they are on.

## What I check here

**Existing leases**, for any income property. Below-market rents cannot be freely corrected, and the return calculation has to account for that from the start.

**The real local vacancy rate**, which varies considerably between streets near the CEGEP and the outskirts.

**System age** in Vieux-Sainte-Thérèse: roof, electrical service, plumbing, French drain.

## Selling and buying in Sainte-Thérèse

For a single-family seller, preparation and list price matter more than the calendar. For a plex seller, the numbers decide: a building with clear financial documents sells faster and higher.

I prepare the comparative analysis at no charge. For a buyer or investor, the return analysis happens before the visit, not after: the income printed in a listing is not a return.
