Saint-Basile-le-Grand is a city of family homes between Mont Saint-Bruno and the Richelieu. According to the 2021 census, 69% of dwellings are single-detached houses and 87% of households own their home. It is an area I cover on the same footing as the others.

## A market shaped by the station and the farmland

More than 80% of the territory is in the permanent agricultural zone. Residential development is concentrated in the urban perimeter, where most homes date from the 1970s to the 1990s.

**The central district**, the village core, is made up mostly of single-family homes built before 1980.

**The station district** has direct access to the train on the exo Mont-Saint-Hilaire line.

**Domaine du Lac**, launched in 1993, Domaine Trinité and Boisé du Golf are the more recent developments.

Mont-Saint-Bruno National Park touches the city limits, with an access point on rang des Vingt.

## What to check here

**The age of the systems** in homes from the 1970s and 1980s: roof, windows, electrical and French drain.

**Flood zones** along the Richelieu, mapped by the MRC de La Vallée-du-Richelieu.

**The environmental history** for a property next to a former industrial site.

**The future of the large industrial site** shared with McMasterville. It was put up for sale after the Northvolt project was abandoned, and its future use may affect nearby properties: find out where the file stands before buying close by.
