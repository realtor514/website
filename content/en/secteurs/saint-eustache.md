Saint-Eustache combines one of the most significant heritage cores in the Laurentians, an extensive waterfront and family residential sectors more affordable than Blainville or Rosemère.

## Three market logics

**Vieux-Saint-Eustache** is the historic heart, around the church and the manor. Older properties, narrow streets, real character. It is also the sector where inspection matters most, because the age of the stock means systems are often at the end of their useful life.

**The waterfront**, along the Rivière des Mille Îles, is a distinct market: fewer buyers, steady demand, and regulatory constraints to verify case by case.

**The family sectors** (Grand-Chêne, Arthur-Sauvé, Rivière-Nord) carry the volume, with homes built mostly between 1980 and 2010.

## What to prioritize here

**Flood zone mapping**, for any property near the water. It is the single most important point in Saint-Eustache, and the one buyers too often discover after the promise to purchase.

**The urban planning by-law** in the heritage core, before any exterior renovation project.

**System age** in older properties: roof, electrical service, plumbing, French drain.
