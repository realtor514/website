Chambly combines a genuine heritage core, a body of water that structures the city, and newer residential sectors more affordable than the immediate South Shore average. It is an area I cover on the same footing as the others.

## A market organized around the water

The Chambly basin is not a landscape detail: it is the main segmentation factor of the local market. Riverfront or view properties trade on a completely different logic from the rest of the city, with fewer buyers but demand that is relatively insensitive to cycles.

**Vieux-Chambly** offers heritage character and proximity to the fort. Properties are older, with the building realities that implies.

**Fonrouge, Domaine des Érables and the Bourgogne sectors** carry the family volume: homes built mostly since 1985, residential streets, reliable comparables.

## What I prioritize here

**Flood zone mapping** along the Richelieu, for any property near the water.

**Drainage and the water table** in sectors developed on former farmland.

**Septic systems and wells** for outlying properties not connected to municipal services.

## Selling and buying in Chambly

The buyer pool is smaller than on the immediate South Shore, which makes the initial list price more decisive: an overpriced property does not correct quickly here.
