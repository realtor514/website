Candiac has been a planned city since its founding in 1957, and it shows in the structure of its market: little disorderly development, homogeneous housing stock by sector, more reliable comparables than in most neighbouring cities.

## What supports prices

Rigorous urban planning produced coherent sectors, with preserved green space and concentrated commercial arteries. The school reputation attracts a family clientele that moves specifically for the sector.

Add to that the exo station on the Candiac line, which gives direct access to downtown Montreal without driving over a bridge. That is a concrete argument for a specific buyer segment.

## The sectors

**Candiac-Est and Candiac-Ouest** form the established residential core, with properties from the 1970s through the 2000s and mature lots.

**Square Candiac** is the recent development hub, with townhouses, condos and governed architectural standards.

**The station sector** carries a transit premium concentrated within a short radius.

## What I check here

**The construction warranty** for recent Square Candiac properties.

**Condo fees and the state of the contingency fund** for condos and co-owned townhouses. Artificially low fees at the start mean a special assessment later.

**System age** in the established sectors: roof, windows, French drain.

## Selling and buying in Candiac

For a Square Candiac seller, the competition includes new construction. For a seller in an established sector, inventory is thinner and preparation matters more than timing.
