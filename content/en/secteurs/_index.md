A real estate market is not read at the scale of a province. It is read street by street. The median price of a semi-detached home in Sainte-Rose tells you nothing useful about what is happening in Chomedey, and a triplex in Old Terrebonne does not negotiate like a triplex in Repentigny.

That is why I work by sector rather than by broad territory. I cover fourteen cities equally, with no preferred territory, across the island of Montreal, the North Shore and the South Shore. My RE/MAX Du Cartier office is in Laval, but I work on the ground in every one of them.

**North Shore and Laurentians:** Laval, Terrebonne, Blainville, Repentigny, Saint-Jérôme.
**Island of Montreal:** all 19 boroughs.
**South Shore and Montérégie:** Longueuil, Brossard, Boucherville, Saint-Bruno-de-Montarville, Chambly, La Prairie, Candiac, Delson.

Each page below describes what I actually know about the area: the neighbourhoods, the dominant property types, what moves prices locally, and the mistakes I see most often from buyers and sellers.

I work in French, English, Spanish and Arabic. If your area is not on the list, write to me anyway: I serve Greater Montreal, the Laurentians and Lanaudière.
