Repentigny is the gateway to Lanaudière for most buyers coming from the east end of the island. It is a market I cover regularly, partly because a significant share of my Montreal clientele ends up there.

## A market pulled by east-end Montreal

Unlike Terrebonne or Blainville, whose clientele comes largely from Laval and the north, Repentigny mainly receives buyers from Rivière-des-Prairies, Pointe-aux-Trembles, Montréal-Nord and Anjou.

That has a direct consequence on prices: the Repentigny market tracks east-island dynamics closely. When prices rise in Rivière-des-Prairies, the pressure shifts to Repentigny in the months that follow.

For a seller, this means the right comparison is not purely local. For a buyer, it means you need to watch both markets in parallel before concluding that one is the better deal.

## The sectors

**Le Gardeur**, merged into Repentigny in 2002, keeps its own identity and price structure. Properties are often more affordable at comparable square footage, making it one of the most interesting entry points for a first purchase in Lanaudière.

**Old Repentigny** is the oldest sector and the closest to services. Mature lots, established trees, properties mostly from the 1960s through the 1980s. It is also the sector where inspection matters most, because the age of the housing stock means several properties have systems at the end of their useful life.

**The riverfront sector** is a distinct market. Fewer buyers, but steady demand that is relatively insensitive to general fluctuations. Regulatory constraints tied to shoreline buffers and flood zones must be verified property by property: two neighbouring homes can have different statuses.

## What I prioritize here

**Flood zone mapping**, for any property near the river or the L'Assomption. It affects insurability, financing and resale. That is not a reason not to buy, it is a reason to know exactly what you are buying.

**System age**, in Old Repentigny especially: roof, windows, electrical panel, water heater. A property with four systems reaching end of life at the same time is not worth what an otherwise identical property is worth after that work is done.

**Septic systems and wells**, for outlying properties not connected to municipal services.

## Selling in Repentigny

The list price has to account for both buyer pools: local buyers and those coming from the east end of the island. The latter compare your property to what they would have gotten in Rivière-des-Prairies for the same amount, and that comparison often decides the offer.
