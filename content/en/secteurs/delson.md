Delson is a small city in the Roussillon area, between Candiac, Saint-Constant and [Sainte-Catherine](/en/real-estate-broker/sainte-catherine/). It had 8,328 residents at Statistics Canada's 2021 census, 12% more than in 2016, according to Centris. In the third quarter of 2026, the single-family median in its APCIQ sector was below the median for the South Shore as a whole.

## Delson by the numbers

Centris publishes statistics for Delson alone, but with few sales per quarter. The most stable figure is the one for the last four quarters.

The APCIQ groups Delson with Saint-Constant, Sainte-Catherine, Saint-Mathieu and Saint-Philippe in sector 40, "Sud-ouest de la Rive-Sud". Figures for this sector cover five cities, not Delson alone.

Three readings. First, price: in the same quarter, the single-family median was $750,000 in the Candiac/La Prairie sector and $645,000 for the South Shore as a whole. Second, supply: active single-family listings in the sector rose 25%, to 201. Third, the balance of power: over the last 12 months, the APCIQ classifies the sector's single-family market as a seller's market below $910,000 (2.9 to 6.8 months of inventory) and as balanced at $910,000 and above (10.0 months). The APCIQ notes that a median price does not reflect the value of each property, especially when sales are few.

## Property taxes, assessment roll and welcome tax

Delson's 2025-2026-2027 assessment roll, prepared by LBP Évaluateurs agréés, reflects the market on July 1, 2023. According to the City, the average assessment of a single-family home is $497,127.

For transfer duties, the City applies a 3% rate in 2026 on the portion above $500,000, on top of the provincial brackets of 0.5%, 1% and 1.5%. The tax base is the higher of the sale price and the standardized value (roll value multiplied by the comparative factor, 1.08 in 2026). For a house sold at $585,500, the calculation gives $8,175.50. Provincial thresholds change in 2027: details are in the [welcome tax guide](/en/articles/welcome-tax-quebec-guide/).

## Transit and schools

Delson station, at 1, chemin de la Gare, is on exo's 14 Candiac train line, in fare zone C. According to exo, it has 102 parking spaces.

Delson is served by the Centre de services scolaire des Grandes-Seigneuries. Its 2026-2027 enrolment rule places Delson in the same group as Sainte-Catherine and Saint-Constant. The school for a specific address is confirmed with the school service centre.

## What to check here

**System age** for any house built before 1990: roof, electrical service, windows, French drain.

**Connection to municipal services**, for outlying properties that may still run on a septic system or a well.

**Rail proximity**, which is an advantage for the station but a noise factor for some properties along the track. It is negotiable, provided you know before the offer.
