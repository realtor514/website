Delson is a small city in the Roussillon area, wedged between Candiac, Saint-Constant and Sainte-Catherine. It is often the best entry point in the area for a buyer whose budget does not stretch to Candiac.

## A local market

Delson does not have Candiac's urban planning or La Prairie's heritage. What it has is varied housing stock, often larger lots at a comparable price, and an exo station within the city.

The market is essentially single-family, with a few semi-detached homes and a limited number of income properties. Transaction volume is low, and that has a direct consequence: comparables are fewer, and a single atypical sale can distort an automated estimate.

This is exactly the kind of market where a manual analysis beats an algorithm.

## What to check here

**System age**, because part of the stock dates from the 1960s through the 1980s: roof, electrical service, windows, French drain.

**Connection to municipal services**, for outlying properties that may still run on a septic system or a well.

**Rail proximity**, which is an advantage for the station but a noise factor for some properties along the track. It is negotiable, provided you know before the offer.

## Selling and buying in Delson

With low transaction volume, the initial list price matters even more than elsewhere: there are fewer buyers to absorb a mistake, and an overpriced property sits.

The comparative analysis is prepared at no charge, widening to neighbouring cities when local comparables are insufficient and adjusting for the market difference.
