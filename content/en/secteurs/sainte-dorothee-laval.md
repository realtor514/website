Sainte-Dorothée occupies the southwest of Île Jésus, bordered by Laval-sur-le-Lac to the west, Laval-Ouest to the northwest, Fabreville to the northeast, Chomedey to the east, and by Îles-Laval and the rivière des Prairies to the south. It is one of the fourteen neighbourhoods on my [Laval page](/en/real-estate-broker/laval/), and the one whose conditions have changed most in two years.

## What moves prices here

Three layers overlap here.

**Agriculture.** The north of the sector is in the permanent agricultural zone, dominated by greenhouses and horticultural production. The Route des Fleurs, a circuit of about ten kilometres linking the growers, runs through it (Tourisme Laval). The bois de Sainte-Dorothée, owned by the city, is open in winter only, from December 1 to March 31 (Ville de Laval).

**Residential history.** The parish was created in 1869, residential neighbourhoods appeared in the 1950s near the village core, and the population of the former municipality nearly tripled between 1950 and 1965. Laval-sur-le-Lac separated from it in 1915, Îles-Laval in 1941 (Ville de Laval).

**The train.** The REM's Sainte-Dorothée station, at 1411 chemin du Bord-de-l'Eau, has been in service since November 17, 2025 on the Deux-Montagnes branch (REM). It is the most recent factor, and the most visible one in new construction.

## The micro-areas

**Vieux-Sainte-Dorothée** is the village core, around the public square and the 1873 church built to the plans of architect Victor Bourgeau (Tourisme Laval). Late 19th century houses line rue Principale (Ville de Laval).

**Chemin du Bord-de-l'Eau** follows the rivière des Prairies and retains traditional Québécois houses (Ville de Laval). This is where the flood zone question arises most directly, and where the REM station sits.

**The station area** is moving fastest, with recent construction, including projects built on former golf course land. A new build and an 1980s single-family home on the same street do not target the same buyers.

**The Route des Fleurs and rang Saint-Antoine** border the agricultural zone, with mansard-roofed houses along the rang (Ville de Laval). Buying here means accepting seasonal farm traffic.

**Boulevard Samson** and the edges of Autoroute 13 form the commercial hub. Road access runs through Autoroute 13, known as Chomedey, and Autoroute 440.

Immediately to the south, Îles-Laval is a separate sector, served by the REM's Île-Bigras station.

## Getting around

From Sainte-Dorothée station, the REM reaches Gare Centrale with no transfer, with access to the metro's blue line at Édouard-Montpetit and the green line at McGill. The station has 836 parking spaces, 671 of them free (REM), and the STL runs routes 26, 76, 203, 713 and 903 through it.

## Flood zones: the verification that matters most

As Ville de Laval describes it: the new government regulatory framework came into force on March 1, 2026, but the mapping currently in force, high-velocity zones of 0 to 20 years and low-velocity zones of 20 to 100 years, remains the reference until the new one is published. The flood levels applied come from a study completed in 2014 and approved by the Government of Quebec. The Communauté métropolitaine de Montréal is producing the new Laval mapping, whose publication is expected by the end of 2026.

Concrete consequence: the map that applies to a waterfront address can change while you own the property, with possible effects on insurance, financing and the right to rebuild.

What a buyer does before signing a promise to purchase, in this order:

1. Consult Ville de Laval's maps: recurrence zones of 2, 20 and 100 years, the highest known water level, and the map of the territory flooded in 2017 and 2019.
2. Have the city confirm the situation of the exact address, never a real estate site's map.
3. Require an up-to-date certificate of location, later than the last work done: it flags the flood zone and the servitudes.
4. Get the insurer's and the lender's position in writing before the conditions expire.

Zoning is checked in parallel: the [Laval buying guide](/en/articles/buying-in-laval-2025/) details the Info-règlements portal and the other municipal verifications.

## Schools

The sector falls under the Centre de services scolaire de Laval, which operates école Sainte-Dorothée among others. An address's catchment is set by the school service centre and can be revised: use its Trouver son école tool with the exact address. The English network falls under the Sir Wilfrid Laurier School Board. See also [schools and property values](/en/articles/school-districts-property-values/).
