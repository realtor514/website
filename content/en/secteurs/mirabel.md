Mirabel is the largest and most fragmented territory in the region. It is also one of the cities where an automated estimate is most likely to be wrong.

## One city, ten markets

Mirabel is the result of merging about ten former villages separated by agricultural land. These are not neighbourhoods: they are distinct cores, sometimes fifteen kilometres apart.

**Saint-Janvier** is the most developed hub and the closest to Highway 15. Most recent construction and services are concentrated there.

**Domaine Vert Nord** is a planned residential sector adjacent to the Domaine Vert park, with homogeneous housing stock.

**Saint-Canut** has grown quickly and still offers larger lots at lower prices.

**Sainte-Scholastique, Saint-Augustin and Saint-Benoît** remain rural, with properties on large lots and sometimes agricultural uses.

Comparing a Saint-Janvier sale to a Saint-Benoît property makes no sense. That is exactly what online tools do.

## What I check here

**Agricultural zoning**, before any offer on a rural property. Construction, expansion and subdivision possibilities are strictly governed.

**Septic systems and wells**, very common outside the urban cores. Their age and compliance are verified beforehand, not after.

**The construction warranty** in the recent Saint-Janvier and Saint-Canut developments.
