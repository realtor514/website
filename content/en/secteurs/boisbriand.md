Boisbriand is a city in the Thérèse-De Blainville RCM, on the north shore of the Rivière des Mille Îles. It had 28,308 residents at Statistics Canada's 2021 census, according to Centris. With the development of the Faubourg, built on a former industrial site, the city now has two market logics that coexist.

## Two markets in one city

**Faubourg Boisbriand** is a planned neighbourhood with architectural requirements, a commercial core and higher density than the rest of the city. It has townhouses, condos and recent single-family homes. For a reseller, the competition often includes inventory still under construction.

**The established sectors** (Chambéry, Boisbriand-Est, Grande-Côte) are markets of older single-family homes on larger lots. Comparables there are reliable because the properties are similar enough.

## Assessment roll and welcome tax

The 2026-2027-2028 assessment roll is the basis of the 2026 budget. According to the City, the average value of a residential property rises 23% on this roll, to $592,866. Assessment notices were sent with the tax bill on January 30, 2026.

For transfer duties, since January 1, 2026, Boisbriand applies a two-step scale above $500,000: 2.5% on the portion from $500,000 to $800,000, then 3% on the portion above $800,000. Example published by the City: $6,860.50 for a $550,000 property. Under the same scale, a tax base of $800,000 gives $13,110.50. The full calculation and the 2027 provincial thresholds are in the [welcome tax guide](/en/articles/welcome-tax-quebec-guide/).

## Transit, schools and the rental market

**Autoroute 15.** A lane reserved for carpooling and public transit is under construction northbound between Boisbriand and Mirabel. In 2026, work is concentrated between Route 344 in Boisbriand and exit 25 in Blainville; full service is planned for the end of 2027, according to the Ministère des Transports et de la Mobilité durable. The Gédéon-Ouimet bridge, which links Laval and Boisbriand, will be rebuilt; the project is in planning, with no published cost or schedule.

**Train.** No station of the 12 Saint-Jérôme line is in Boisbriand. The neighbouring Sainte-Thérèse station, at 6, rue de la Gare, has 573 parking spaces.

**Schools.** Boisbriand is served by the Centre de services scolaire des Mille-Îles. Its schools in Boisbriand include Le Sentier, Le Tandem, Gabrielle-Roy, Gaston-Pilon, du Mai and Plateau Saint-Louis.

**Rentals.** The rental vacancy rate was 2.9% in October 2025 (grade c), compared with 0.5% a year earlier, according to CMHC. From January to September 2026, CMHC counted 68 housing starts in Boisbriand, compared with 198 in the same period of 2025.
