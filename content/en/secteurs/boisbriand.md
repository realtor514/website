Boisbriand changed profile with the development of the Faubourg, built on the former industrial site. The city now holds two market logics side by side.

## Two markets in one city

**Faubourg Boisbriand** is a planned district with architectural requirements, a commercial core and higher density than the rest of the city. It holds townhouses, condos and recent single-family homes. For a reseller, the competition often includes inventory still under construction.

**The established sectors** (Chambéry, Boisbriand-Est, Grande-Côte) are single-family markets from the 1970s through the 2000s, with larger lots and a stable family clientele. Comparables here are reliable because properties resemble each other enough.

## What I check here

**The residential construction warranty** for Faubourg properties under ten years old: what is still covered, what is not, what has already been claimed.

**Condo fees and the state of the contingency fund** for townhouses and condos. Low fees in year one often mean a special assessment later.

**System age** in the established sectors: roof, windows, French drain.

## Selling and buying in Boisbriand

The list price has to reflect the sector's logic, not the municipal average. A Faubourg property and a Chambéry property do not attract the same buyer.
