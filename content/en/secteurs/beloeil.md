Beloeil sits on the west bank of the Richelieu, facing Mont-Saint-Hilaire, and Highway 20 runs through it. The city had 24,104 residents at the 2021 census. It is an area I cover on the same footing as the others.

## A more varied market than it looks

According to the 2021 census, 59% of dwellings are single-detached houses and 29% are apartments in buildings under five storeys. The stock also includes many older homes: 38% of dwellings date from 1961 to 1980, and 13% from before 1961.

**Vieux-Beloeil**, along rue Richelieu and rue Saint-Jean-Baptiste, is the heritage core. It has its own special urban plan and a heritage trail of 45 panels.

**The rue Richelieu sector** borders the river. The view of the water and of Mont Saint-Hilaire is a concrete selling point there.

**Nouveau Beloeil**, a recent and denser residential neighbourhood, and **Faubourg Richelieu**, along Highway 20, make up the newer supply.

## What to check here

**Heritage rules** in Vieux-Beloeil. Exterior renovations may require architectural approval: check before buying with work in mind.

**Flood zones** along the Richelieu, for any property near the water.

**The age of the systems** in homes from the 1960s and 1970s: roof, windows, French drain, type of electrical wiring and the possible presence of asbestos in some materials.

Buyers compare Beloeil with [Mont-Saint-Hilaire](/en/real-estate-broker/mont-saint-hilaire/), across the river, and with [Saint-Basile-le-Grand](/en/real-estate-broker/saint-basile-le-grand/). In Vieux-Beloeil, buyers pay for character, but the condition of the building sets the ceiling.
