Residential real estate broker at RE/MAX DU CARTIER INC., OACIQ licence J7941.
Here is what a mandate actually covers, from the first call to the signature at
the notary.

## Selling a property

Pricing based on recent, verified comparable sales rather than a range given by
feel. File preparation: certificate of location, seller's declarations,
co-ownership documents, work invoices. Professional photography, Centris
listing, distribution across the RE/MAX network and social media. Organizing
and attending visits. Analysis of each offer, its conditions and the strength
of the buyer's financing, then negotiation. Follow-up on conditions through to
signature.

The steps are set out on the [seller's page](/en/seller/).

## Buying a property

Setting the real budget, down payment and closing costs included, before the
first visit. Searches and alerts on listings matching the mandate, including
those not yet widely circulated. Visits, critical reading of the seller's
declaration and the co-ownership documents. Drafting the promise to purchase,
the inspection and financing conditions, then negotiation. Coordination with
the inspector, the mortgage broker and the notary.

The steps are set out on the [buyer's page](/en/buyer/).

## Assessing market value

Free assessment with no obligation, based on comparable sales in the area and a
visit to the property. It serves equally for preparing a sale, deciding on a
refinancing or settling an estate. The
[online estimator](/en/tools/home-estimate/) gives a first order of magnitude
in seconds.

## First-time buyers

Step-by-step guidance: borrowing capacity, applicable government programs,
transfer duties, closing costs, choosing an area, reading an inspection report.
The six [calculators on this site](/en/tools/) support those discussions.

## Income properties and investment

Profitability analysis of a plex or an income building: gross revenue, real
expenses, multiplier, return on down payment, state of the leases and the rent
register. Reading the zoning and the municipality's constraints.

## Estates, separations, special mandates

Sales in the context of an estate or a division of property, with the delays
and documents those files require, and coordination with the notary handling
the settlement.

## What comes with the agency

Three RE/MAX Québec protections, at no extra charge: Tranquilli-T for legal
assistance, Integri-T for latent defects and Coproprie-T for co-ownership. The
detail is on the [advantages page](/en/advantages/).

## Territory

Laval, Montreal, the North Shore, the South Shore, the Laurentians and
Lanaudière. The site covers 38 cities, each with its own page.
