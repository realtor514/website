## Using this site creates no brokerage relationship

This is the most important point on this page. Reading an article, using a
calculator or sending a message through the form creates no brokerage contract
and no obligation on either side.

A brokerage relationship arises only from a signed written brokerage contract,
on the mandatory OACIQ form. Until that contract is signed, no step is taken on
your behalf and no fee is owed.

## The calculators give orders of magnitude

The six calculators on this site apply the Quebec rules in force as of their
last update: transfer duty brackets, CMHC premiums, GDS and TDS ratios, tax
rates.

Their results are estimates. They are not a mortgage pre-approval, a certified
appraisal, tax advice or a financing offer. A lender decides on your full
credit file. A market value is established from recent comparable sales and a
visit. Scales and rates change, and a page may not have been updated on the day
you read it.

Check any figure that matters with your lender, your notary or your accountant
before committing.

## The articles are not professional advice

Articles on this site regularly deal with taxation, latent defects, insurance,
co-ownership and real estate law. They provide general information, current as
of their publication date, written for a broad audience.

They do not replace the advice of a notary, a lawyer, an accountant, a
chartered appraiser or a building inspector. Every situation has its
particulars, and a general rule that is accurate can give a wrong result when
applied to a specific case.
