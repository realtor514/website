In the third quarter of 2026, single-family homes sold at a median price of
$835,000 in Blainville, compared with $710,000 in Sainte-Thérèse and $622,500 in
Boisbriand, according to Centris. In the four other towns of the MRC de
Thérèse-De Blainville, too few sales took place for a quarterly median price to
be published. Below are the figures for all seven towns, followed by three
concrete differences between them: transfer duties, the train and the Rivière
des Mille Îles.

## Seven towns compared, and set against the Laurentides

**Prices.** Over four quarters, the single-family median ranges from $528,566
in Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines to $877,722 in Rosemère. Only Bois-des-Filion and
Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines remain below the Laurentides median of $567,487. For
condos, where it is published in four towns, it ranges from $366,250 in
Sainte-Thérèse to $492,237 in Boisbriand.

**Time on market.** In the quarter, single-family homes sold in 28 to 38 days
on average in the three largest towns, compared with 49 days for the
Laurentides as a whole.

**What APCIQ says.** Its barometer groups Boisbriand and Sainte-Thérèse in
sector 27, Blainville, Lorraine and Rosemère in sector 28, and places
Bois-des-Filion and Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines with Terrebonne. Over the last 12
months, every single-family price range in sector 28 is classified "Seller", at
1.9 to 7.1 months of inventory. In sector 27, only the range of $940,000 and up
falls outside that category: 9.8 months, classified "Balanced". APCIQ places a
balanced market between 8 and 10 months.

## What changed in a year

For the metropolitan area, APCIQ counts 9,265 residential sales in the quarter
(-12%) and notes that the North Shore remains to the advantage of sellers, for
single-family homes as well as for condos. The Bank of Canada's policy rate is
unchanged at 2.25% as of October 21, 2026; the next announcement is scheduled
for October 28.

**Single-family homes held up in the three largest towns**: sales rose 8% in
Blainville and 16% in Sainte-Thérèse and were stable in Boisbriand, while total
residential sales fell in each of them (-4%, -9% and -7%).

**Three smaller towns slowed down**: 38 residential sales in Rosemère (-32%), 34
in Bois-des-Filion (-17%) and 31 in Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines (-20%). Over four
quarters, Boisbriand shows the steepest decline: 238 sales (-32%), including 62
condos (-47%).

**Supply grew in the six towns where Centris publishes the change**: active
listings, all categories combined, rose by anywhere from 8% in Blainville to
48% in Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines, compared with 15% for the Laurentides.

**Price increases, to be read with caution.** The +15% in Sainte-Thérèse and the
+11% in Blainville cover a single quarter; over four quarters, the increases are
5% and 4%. A change in the median price is not the change in value of a given
house, because the mix of properties sold changes from one quarter to the next.

## Getting around: the train, Highway 15 and Highway 19

**The train.** The exo Saint-Jérôme line, line 12, stops at three stations in
the area, in fare zone C: Blainville (765 parking spaces), Sainte-Thérèse (573)
and Rosemère (323). Boisbriand, Lorraine, Bois-des-Filion and
Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines have no station on that line.

**Highway 19.** The Ministère des Transports et de la Mobilité durable
(Quebec's transport ministry) is extending it by 11.8 km, from Laval to
boulevard Industriel in Bois-des-Filion, where a bus terminus with a
park-and-ride lot is planned at the interchange of Highways 19 and 640.
According to its page updated on October 2, 2026, the new bridge over the
Rivière des Mille Îles should be completed in the fall of 2026, but full
commissioning is "under reassessment." No official source consulted puts a
number on the effect of this project on prices.

**Highway 15.** A first section of lane reserved for carpooling and public
transit, northbound, has been open since December 2025 between boulevard de la
Seigneurie Ouest and Highway 50. In 2026, the work is concentrated between
Route 344 in Boisbriand and exit 25 in Blainville; full commissioning is
scheduled for the end of 2027. The Gédéon-Ouimet bridge, between Laval and
Boisbriand, will be rebuilt, with no cost or timeline published.

## What sets Blainville apart from Bois-des-Filion and the others

### Three transfer duty regimes

In 2026, Sainte-Thérèse, Rosemère, Lorraine, Bois-des-Filion and
Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines tax the portion above $500,000 at 3% for the transfer
duties (droits de mutation, the welcome tax). Boisbriand charges 2.5% from
$500,000 to $800,000, then 3%. Blainville, in its 2026 budget, brought the rate
on the portion from $500,000 to $750,000 down to 1.5% to make home ownership
more accessible.

| Price | Blainville | Boisbriand | The five other towns |
|---|---|---|---|
| $650,000 | $7,860.50 | $9,360.50 | $10,110.50 |
| $850,000 | $12,360.50 | $14,610.50 | $16,110.50 |

Calculated with the 2026 rates published by each town, assuming a tax base equal
to the price. Blainville itself publishes the $850,000 example: $12,360.50 in
2026, compared with $16,153.50 in 2025. The actual base is the higher of the
price and the standardized value, that is, the municipal assessment multiplied
by the comparative factor, which is 1.12 in Rosemère in 2026. Redo the
calculation with the [welcome tax calculator](/en/tools/welcome-tax/).

### New housing, mostly in Blainville

From January to September 2026, CMHC recorded 576 housing starts in Blainville,
including 534 apartments, compared with 223 a year earlier; Boisbriand fell from
198 to 68. A housing start is a unit on which construction has begun, not one
that has been delivered. On the rental side, the October 2025 survey, the most
recent, puts the vacancy rate at 2.9% in Boisbriand (rating c), compared with
0.5% a year earlier, and at 1.4% in Sainte-Thérèse. CMHC does not publish the
overall rate for Blainville or for Rosemère.

### Water: the river and heavy rain

The first batch of new-generation flood zone maps from the Communauté
métropolitaine de Montréal (CMM) covers the banks of the Rivière des Mille
Îles. On July 30, 2026, the CMM asked the Ministère de l'Environnement (Quebec's
environment ministry) to reverse the postponement of their publication to an
undetermined date. The government page on the maps in force, updated on August
28, 2026, announces that the new maps will be published gradually "over the
coming months"; until then, the maps of the municipality or of the MRC apply.

As of October 21, 2026, Rosemère's page still describes the transitional
regime, which Quebec replaced on March 1, 2026. For an address near the river,
put the question in writing to the town's urban planning department, and read
about [buying in a flood zone in Quebec](/en/articles/buying-in-flood-zone-quebec/).

Against heavy rain, Rosemère has offered free support to eligible properties
since July 2026, together with the MRC: inspection, report, quote and
compliance visit. Inspections are spread out until November 1, 2027, and the
work itself remains the owner's responsibility.

## Selling in the area

**In Blainville**, 174 single-family homes were on the market for 148 sales in
the quarter. The quarterly median, $835,000, is above the median for the last
four quarters, $762,625, and neither one sets the price of a specific house.
See how to [audit real estate comparables](/en/articles/real-estate-comparables-quebec/).

**In Sainte-Thérèse and Boisbriand**, the inventory of single-family homes
jumped (+83% and +50%) while sales held steady. Buyers have more houses to
compare: base your asking price on recent, comparable sales.

**In Rosemère and Lorraine**, the analysis is done sale by sale, widening it if
needed to Blainville, which is in the same APCIQ sector. If your Rosemère house
has had its inspection against rainfall flooding, keep the report and the
invoices.

**For a plex in Sainte-Thérèse** (72 sales in a year), prepare the leases and
the occupancy history.

## Buying in Blainville, Sainte-Thérèse or Boisbriand

**The budget.** In Blainville, Boisbriand, Lorraine and Bois-des-Filion, the
transfer duties are paid in a single instalment. Blainville sends the bill
about 60 days after the deed is registered by the notary; the three others make
it payable within 30 days of the bill being sent.

**The entry price.** Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines and Bois-des-Filion have the lowest
single-family median prices over four quarters. In Sainte-Anne-des-Plaines, the
choice is widening: 46 single-family homes on the market, 58% more than a year
earlier.

**Condos.** Over four quarters, among the four towns where Centris publishes
them, Sainte-Thérèse has the lowest median price, $366,250, and the shortest
time on market, 33 days.

**The address.** Check the flood zone map near the river, ask for the history
of water damage, and drive the commute at rush hour, construction included.

## Frequently asked questions

**Did houses in Sainte-Thérèse gain 15% in a year?** No. It is the median price
of the 35 single-family homes sold in the third quarter that rose 15%. Over four
quarters, the increase is 5%, to $615,400.

**When will Highway 19 reach Bois-des-Filion?** There is no official date: the
ministry states that the timeline for full commissioning is under reassessment.

**Why is the municipal assessment so far from the price?** Each assessment roll
reflects the market at a past date specific to the town. Rosemère's is based on
July 1, 2022 and is in its last year in 2026; Blainville's, on July 1, 2023;
those of Sainte-Thérèse and Boisbriand, in force since 2026, on July 1, 2024.
