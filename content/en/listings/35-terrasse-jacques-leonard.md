Three-level townhouse in Domaine Bonneville, an enclave of 250 homes on a mature landscaped site in Rivière-des-Prairies. Private garage in the basement, exclusive driveway, private fenced backyard.

## What the domain adds

Residents have access to reserved common areas, which explains part of the practical value of a townhouse at this price:

- **Pool** and **wading pool**
- **Private park** and **playground**
- **Community centre**

Common charges cover insurance, the roof, masonry, balconies, snow removal and the contingency fund. The exact amount is not published on the listing sheet; it is provided on request.

## The layout

Nine rooms spread over three levels. The main floor holds the living room, dining room, kitchen and a powder room. The upper floor has three bedrooms and a full bathroom. The basement is fully finished: kitchen, living room, fourth bedroom, second full bathroom and the garage.

At the back, the fenced yard includes a covered deck and a shed.
