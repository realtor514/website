Country home built in 1935, on a 22,152 sq ft lot in the farming heart of Saint-Hermas, Mirabel. Nine rooms over two floors, a wooden barn on the property, and open views over the fields with no facing neighbour.

The asking price of $449,000 sits below the 2026 municipal assessment of $507,400.

## What has been done

The expensive items have been redone recently, which removes part of the usual uncertainty on a house of this age:

- **Vinyl exterior cladding** fully replaced in 2024, with municipal permit
- **Central heat pump** installed in 2022
- **New generator** installed in 2025, connected to heating and cooling
- **Well, septic tank and water filtration system**
- **Water softener and sediment filter**, serviced in December 2025
- **200 amp electrical panel**
- **Attic insulated** with blown cellulose, PVC sash windows

## The layout

Nine rooms over two floors, for 2,379 sq ft of living space. The main floor holds the entrance hall, living room, dining room, kitchen, primary bedroom, a full bathroom and the laundry room. A slow-combustion wood stove heats the living space.

The upper floor has three more bedrooms, a second full bathroom and a second kitchen. This two-kitchen layout, one per floor, suits an intergenerational family or shared use of the two levels.

Outside, a large wooden gallery faces the fields. The driveway holds two vehicles.

## The barn

A two-storey wooden barn sits on the property, next to the house. It serves as storage space. A chicken coop completes the outbuildings.
