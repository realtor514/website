Two-storey home on a fenced lot of nearly 4,000 square feet, in a sought-after part of Old Longueuil. Close to services, schools and access routes toward the Jacques-Cartier bridge.

## What has been done

The structural work is complete, which removes a significant part of the uncertainty for a buyer:

- **Roof** redone in 2014
- **Plumbing and electrical** updated in 2014
- **Main floor** sanded in 2026
- **Above-ground pool** installed in 2023
- **Wooden deck**, shed and an **EV charging station**

## The layout

The main floor holds the living room, kitchen, dining room and a powder room. The primary bedroom occupies the upper floor. The basement is finished and includes a second bedroom along with a full bathroom.

The asphalt driveway accommodates two vehicles.
