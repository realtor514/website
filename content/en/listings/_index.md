The properties listed under my name, current or sold, and how to read what
each listing states.

## Why sold properties stay online

A sold listing does not disappear from the site. It keeps its SOLD marking and
remains readable. That is deliberate: a seller looking for a broker wants to
see what actually closed, in which area and in which type of property, not only
what is in the window today.

The price of a sold property is no longer displayed. The OACIQ guideline on
advertising completed transactions prohibits announcing the price a property
sold for, or any figure that would allow it to be deduced. Only the price of an
active listing may be published.

## What each listing holds

Property type, year of construction, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, lot
size, parking, municipal and school taxes, municipal assessment and the Centris
number. The photographs are those of the listing.

This information comes from the seller and from the documents available when
the page went online. It is published in good faith and must be verified before
any decision, as the [terms of use](/en/terms-of-use/) set out.
